The goal of this portfolio is not to list technologies. It is to create confidence.

That means the layout has to feel intentional, the motion has to feel calm, and the content has to explain why the work matters.

```ts
const portfolio = {
  story: "product-first",
  data: "live",
  feel: "premium"
};
```

The next iteration will connect more live data and automate even more of the content.
