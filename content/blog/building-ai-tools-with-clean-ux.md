AI products work best when the interface removes uncertainty.

The strongest experiences show users what is happening, why a result exists, and what they can do next.

```tsx
<button aria-label="Ask AI assistant">Ask</button>
```

This same principle drives the chatbot and case-study structure in the rest of the portfolio.
